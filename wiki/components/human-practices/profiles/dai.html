RNA EDITING & TRANSLATION

Promise needs careful validation
  • RNA editing avoids permanent DNA changes and may offer a more controllable approach.
  • Balance on-target efficiency with off-target risk; protective APOE2/3 support may pair well.
  • Test delivery, specificity and whole-body effects in vivo before clinical translation.
