Local review, 21 October 2026

WORLDPROOF: solving real problems in the real world.
Existing slogan: Build it. Break it. Fix it.
This page companion is not the original event rulebook or final scoring sheet.

BUILD
Use a biology-related problem to make a demonstrable proposal. The available
English challenge PDF contains eight hypothetical scenarios: Riverlight,
FieldShift, FermentOS, ClinicNow, ProBio Home, Mossback, ClearCoast and OpenBench.
They contain conflicts, stakeholders, possible demos and reality-check examples.
The planning document describes project and scenario entrances. Its proposed
support measures must be distinguished from confirmed delivery.

BREAK
Captain-confirmed requirement: each team must act as a red team to challenge
at least three other teams. The planning prompts ask who is affected, what fails,
what follows and what warning can be observed. Target assumptions and systems,
not people. An observer's idea and a documented challenge are distinct evidence.

FIX
Captain-confirmed requirement: each team must respond to at least three
suggestions from other teams and explain improvements in its change log.
The captain reports judges paid particular attention to this. The final scoring
rubric is not yet supplied; no numeric weighting is asserted here. The local
concept draft's suggested percentages must not be used as confirmed final rules.

FOLLOW A PROJECT
The all-group change log exists (captain confirmation), awaiting Feishu transfer.
Match one initial demo, incoming challenges, log entries and final demo. Retain
reasons for non-adoption, unfinished changes or unhelpful feedback if recorded.
Captain's recollection: the first-prize team was mainly mathematics/statistics,
with little biology background; judging included biology expertise. Formal team,
award and judge records have not yet been matched. Do not infer creativity or
access outcomes from academic background or the count of required revisions.

PERMISSIONS AND USE
Check sharing permissions and remove unnecessary identities before posting logs,
video frames or quotations. Recorded material is not automatically approved for
publication. No external reuse or measured participation outcome is asserted.
