Residue identity improves ranking within the measured library

Within the evaluated library, position-only Ridge reached Spearman ρ = 0.155, position-specific amino-acid one-hot Ridge reached 0.292, and the combined one-hot and frozen ESM model reached 0.405. Residue identity and assay-calibrated evolutionary information strengthen the ranking of variants from this measured sequence space.

The resulting ranker integrates residue identity with assay-calibrated protein representations to prioritize recognition-domain variants.

Protein sequence and RNA context guide recognition design

Protein-variant ranking and the exclusive-site context comparisons in Module 4 provide complementary views of recognition design. Engineered-PUF binding experiments show that repeat number and stacking-residue identity affect RNA affinity (Zhao et al., 2018). Together, these analyses link protein-variant ranking with the RNA contexts represented in the measured editing profiles.
