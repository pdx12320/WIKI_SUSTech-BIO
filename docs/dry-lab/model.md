ORCA connects five questions across **transcriptome-wide analysis, protein and RNA foundation models, structural modeling, generative modeling and mechanistic simulation**. The first four stories examine editing profiles, recognition, catalysis and RNA context. Delivery Dynamics continues the story on the Software page, connecting molecular behavior with system-level assumptions.

<ol class="model-result-links" aria-label="Model summaries and overview links">
<li><a href="#module1-1"><span class="result-number">①</span> <strong>Mapping the Editing Landscape</strong></a><small class="model-story-term">Transcriptomic Landscape</small><p>Module 1 asks where editing appears and what fewer detected sites actually mean. RNA-seq profiles are compared with target signals to distinguish changes in editing breadth from reduced activity. These measurements establish the evidence that the following models must explain.</p></li>
<li><a href="#module2-1"><span class="result-number">②</span> <strong>Decoding RNA Recognition</strong></a><small class="model-story-term">Recognition Code</small><p>Module 2 asks whether sequence information can help rank recognition variants. Protein-model features and assay feedback support candidate prioritization, while grouped out-of-fold predictions and feature ablations test the ranking. The evidence describes performance within the measured library, with external transfer still to be assessed.</p></li>
<li><a href="#module3-1"><span class="result-number">③</span> <strong>Balancing Activity and Preference</strong></a><small class="model-story-term">Catalytic Design</small><p>Module 3 asks how catalytic preference relates to editing activity. Structural hypotheses are compared with reporter measurements and matched experimental backgrounds. The resulting story centers on an activity–preference trade-off, while keeping predicted contacts distinct from experimentally measured behavior.</p></li>
<li><a href="#module4-1"><span class="result-number">④</span> <strong>Reading RNA Context</strong></a><small class="model-story-term">LAMAR</small><p>Module 4 asks which RNA sites deserve closer inspection. LAMAR connects region assessment with cytidine ranking, producing shortlists evaluated in positive test windows. These results describe prioritization within the reported partitions; sequence overlap limits claims of independent generalization.</p></li>
<li><a href="/software#rewire-purpose"><span class="result-number">⑤</span> <strong>Following the Delivery Journey</strong></a><small class="model-story-term">Delivery Dynamics</small><p>Delivery Dynamics asks how system-level assumptions shape the outcome beyond a cellular assay. Whole-body ODEs and spatial simulations describe product composition and tissue coverage. Existing perturbation and uncertainty plots show dependence on model assumptions; their outputs remain simulations without experimental calibration.</p></li>
</ol>
