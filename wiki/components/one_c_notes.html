About this RNA schematic

ORCA explores C-to-U RNA editing at a target position in APOE4 messenger RNA. Changing CGC to UGC is intended to change Arg112 to Cys112 in the apoE protein made from this RNA. The other C is outside the intended edit; genomic DNA is not rewritten.

PUF recognizes the target sequence, and APOBEC edits the target C. The animation is a design schematic, with flanking sequence omitted. The coiled RNA is a conceptual molecular illustration, not an experimentally resolved APOE RNA structure.

APOE4 mRNA remains within the astrocyte; translation produces a separate apoE protein. Astrocyte-derived apoE participates in lipid transport to neighbouring cells, including neurons. The intended change at residue 112 is Arg to Cys. Editing of RNA and changes in cell function require separate measurements; neither successful editing nor functional improvement is established by this schematic.

Cell and protein shapes, transport paths and relative sizes are conceptual. Background: our project Description. Residue-112 comparison: ApoE isoform research.

The Aβ flight is a spatial illustration outside the cells. It is separate from apoE-associated lipid transport and does not represent a measured molecular trajectory, neuronal uptake or evidence of functional benefit.
