# WORLDPROOF Judging Criteria

Judges review only the **Change Log** and **Final Demo**. The Change Log contains the New Constraint Card, suggestions from other teams, the challenge selected by the team, and its response. The Baseline Demo and separate Challenge Cards are not reviewed by judges.

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Criterion</th>
      <th>What to Assess</th>
      <th>Score Thresholds</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Challenge<br>30 points</strong></td>
      <td>Assess the Change Log. Determine whether the team understood, synthesized, and prioritized one or more specific real-world challenges from the New Constraint Card and peer suggestions. For each prioritized challenge, a strong framing connects an affected stakeholder, a failure mechanism, a likely consequence, and an observable warning signal.</td>
      <td>
        <strong>0-10:</strong> Feedback is copied or listed without a clear priority or failure mechanism.<br>
        <strong>11-20:</strong> One or more plausible challenges are selected, but the explanation of stakeholders, consequences, or warning signals is incomplete.<br>
        <strong>21-30:</strong> The team clearly synthesizes and prioritizes one or more specific, credible challenges, explaining the relevant stakeholder, consequence, and warning signal for each.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Fix<br>45 points</strong></td>
      <td>Assess the Change Log and Final Demo. Determine whether the team clearly explains its response and whether the Final Demo reflects a meaningful design decision. Strong fixes change the workflow, logic, monitoring, permissions, communication, accessibility, escalation, or stop mechanism rather than merely improving appearance.</td>
      <td>
        <strong>0-15:</strong> No meaningful response, or only a cosmetic change with little connection to the prioritized challenges.<br>
        <strong>16-30:</strong> A relevant change is visible, but the reasoning, implementation, testing, or trade-offs are incomplete.<br>
        <strong>31-45:</strong> A substantial, coherent, and testable redesign directly addresses the prioritized challenges and explains the key trade-offs.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>World Readiness<br>25 points</strong></td>
      <td>Assess whether the Final Demo is usable and responsible in its intended context. Consider users and affected non-users, operational feasibility, monitoring, uncertainty, and the conditions under which the system should pause, escalate, roll back, or stop.</td>
      <td>
        <strong>0-8:</strong> No workable real-world pathway or responsibility boundary; major risks are ignored.<br>
        <strong>9-17:</strong> A workable basic approach, but important users, constraints, monitoring, or uncertainty remain underdeveloped.<br>
        <strong>18-25:</strong> A credible, inclusive, and operationally realistic design with explicit limits, monitoring, escalation, and pause/stop conditions.
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

**Scoring rule:** Select one threshold range for each criterion and assign a specific score within that range. Use the lower or middle part of a range when important elements are missing; use the upper end only when the description is fully supported by the Change Log and Final Demo. Thanks for your time!
