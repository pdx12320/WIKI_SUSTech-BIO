ORCA Inclusivity — resources and adaptation notes
Local review edition, 21 October 2026

Perspective
Spectrum and passband are metaphors for changing needs and the ways a setting
routinely accepts participation. They do not measure people or rank identity,
health or ability. These notes accompany the three-part Inclusivity page.
No external reuse of this toolkit is documented.

Across life stages
Curriculum comparison: read the supporting documents and teacher interviews
on /education#classroom-teacher-advice. The report is about document coverage,
not student ability. Short simulated cases, longer electives and practical work
are options for task design; the proposed course has not been trialled.
Workbook: older readers and caregivers participated in the work, as confirmed
by the captain. Use the recorded role when attributing a particular suggestion.
The first Chinese edition and current student/teacher editions are distinct.
Current student p. 5 and first-edition p. 3 show the same body-change topic.
Current Chinese student pp. 47–50 provide Cornell note pages. The particular
forms and four-week logs are later team choices. Revised-reader testing remains
a separate next step.
Offline: download and read or print selected pages. Camera/audio are optional.
Adapt language, type size, spacing, pace and chapter selection with readers.
Retain the meaning of health/safety information and review local adaptations.
Keep completed health/contact/note forms private. No print-cost comparison exists.
Anjia Guardian: the captain confirmed this is the age-friendly Mini Program.
Home Protection is its website's name, not another product. The supplied native
source and independently built website have different functions. The website
uses a fixed illustrated report, local photo preview and same-tab demonstration
states. It makes no AI assessment, real call, notification or cross-device link.
Native cloud recognition needs separate WeChat/model configuration and testing.
Keep sample and failed states clear. Match user-use records to the actual version.

When needs change
Time Capsule's exact question: If I lost my memory, what one sentence would I
leave for myself? Do not replace this with a generic future-self letter. Do not
publish cards, themes or quotations without records and permission.
Aphasia Map: draw an object card, avoid its name and the specified words, and use
gestures or other descriptions to help a partner identify it. It is a communication
game, not a clinical aphasia simulation. Keep detailed participant observations
separate from an explanation of the rule.
Models: the documented materials are existing brain/cell prints and hand-applied
paint. A full fabrication bill, print settings and measured cost are not supplied.
Handling, looking and facilitator explanation need no camera or computer.
TacTune: Python 3.10+, a local server kept running and a compatible browser.
The local handover package bundles dependencies. Use text/menu exploration
without the camera; speech additionally needs a suitable installed local voice.
Camera route: stable view, lighting and assisted region calibration. Recalibrate
when the setup changes. Camera pointing does not measure physical contact.
Keep structure explanations distinct from geometric/colour pitch mapping.
There is no separate recorded-audio collection. Physical encounters, synthetic
software tests, real-camera frames/release and target-user trials have different
records; the last remains incomplete. Actual regions and audio comfort need
intended-user review. Microphone/voice commands are not an established entry.
Rights: original code Apache-2.0; STL, photos and third-party terms differ. Whole
package redistribution is not newly authorized by this page.
Before I Sign: early timed/drag prototype exists. The 21 October narrative/interface
design removes time pressure and proposes revisitable information and point/select
interaction. Full game, two-person service and participant sessions are not confirmed
complete. Do not report the expected completion date as an observed completion.
The game supports understanding/questions/preferences, not capacity assessment
or replacement of formal consent.
